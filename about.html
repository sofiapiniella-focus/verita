photo booth, 2026

I'm Sofia. I built Verità because I kept buying things that weren't worth it. The viral serum. The cream with "collagen" on the jar. A second vitamin C because a video said so. Nobody at the shelf was going to tell me no.

I'm a business analytics student in Miami. I wanted an answer I couldn't get anywhere: for my skin, my budget, my routine — is this actually worth it? So I built the thing that says it. Verità reads the ingredients, reads real reviews, and gives one plain answer. Including no. Especially then.

There's no team, no brand deals, and no one to talk me out of a "not worth it." Every rule below is one I have to live with too.

— Sof

the rules I can't break

  • 01
    No brand pays for a verdict. Ever.No sponsorships, no placements, no "partner scores." There is no price for a "worth it."
  • 02
    Shop links never change a verdict.Some shop links may earn a small commission. The verdict is generated before, and independently of, any link.
  • 03
    Nothing invented.Ingredient lists come from Open Beauty Facts, your label photo, or a web page we name. If none has a list, the app says "unavailable" instead of guessing.
  • 04
    You can check us.Every fresh verdict lists the review sources it read. Open them yourself.

More on how this works, in plain words, on the FAQ. Data questions are answered on the privacy page. Anything else: info@askverita.com. I read every one.
