01 · scan

point at any barcode.

or snap a photo of the bottle, or search it by name. a photo never runs on its own — you confirm what verità found first.

02 · reading

it shows its work.

before it decides, verità reads the ingredient label and checks what people actually say, live. each page appears as it's read, numbered, so you can see what the verdict stands on.

ingredient lists come only from Open Beauty Facts, the label you photograph, or a page verità names. when there isn't one, it says “ingredient list unavailable” — never a guess.

03 · the verdict

then one honest call.

worth it, or not worth it — weighed against your skin, your hair, and what you value. what people love, what to watch out for, and every source it read, one tap away.

  • no brand pays for a verdict. ever.
  • shop links never change a verdict.
  • when the answer is no, it says no.

the CeraVe verdict and its eight sources are real, word for word from the app (last checked Sep 25, 2026). the “for you” line is written for each person, so it isn't shown here.

04 · my beauty bag

keep what you actually use.

add what's on your shelf and verità remembers it, so it can tell you when a new buy would be a duplicate. later it asks: did it turn out worth it? your answer sharpens every verdict after.

05 · build my routine

then put it in order.

morning and night, built only from what's in your bag, plus the categories you're missing. gaps are categories, not products — nobody is selling you anything.
