How we stay honestthe rules we can't break

Brands can't pay for verdicts.

No sponsorships, no placements, no "partner scores." There is no price for a "worth it."

Shop links never change a verdict.

Some buy links earn us a small commission — the verdict is generated before, and independently of, any link.

Verdicts read real reviews.

Fresh verdicts are built from genuine review coverage, and the sources are shown in the app — so you can check us.

Verità is built by one person — Sofia Piniella, a business analytics student in Miami who got tired of buying products that weren't worth it. Every verdict rule on this page is one I have to live with too.
