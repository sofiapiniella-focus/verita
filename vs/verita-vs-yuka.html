Compare · Verità vs Yuka

A verdict for your skin, or a score for everyone.

Yuka is the scan-and-score benchmark, and it's good at what it does: it reads a label and grades a product's health impact out of 100, the same grade for every person who scans it. Verità answers a different question. Not "is this product clean?" but "is this worth it, for me?"

When Yuka is the better pick

If your question is "does this contain something I want to avoid?" Yuka answers it in seconds, for food too. If you want a single number you can compare across a whole shelf, that's a score, and Verità doesn't give scores.

When Verità is

If the product is "clean" and you still don't know whether to buy it. A cleanser can pass every hazard check and still be the wrong one for breakout-prone skin, or a $100 answer to a $12 question.
